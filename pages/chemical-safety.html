Household Guidance

Families, Children & Pets

Exact requirements vary by product and treatment. Always follow the technician’s instructions.

Families

Share health, access or occupancy concerns before treatment so relevant instructions can be provided.

Children

Keep children away from treatment areas for the advised period and secure toys or personal items as directed.

Pets

Tell us about all pets, aquariums and animal areas; follow product-specific relocation and re-entry guidance.

Before Treatment

  • Follow preparation instructions
  • Protect food and personal items
  • Provide access to treatment areas
  • Tell us about pets and sensitivities

During Treatment

  • Respect restricted areas
  • Keep children and pets away
  • Ask questions before application
  • Follow technician directions

After Treatment

  • Observe stated re-entry time
  • Ventilate only as instructed
  • Follow cleaning guidance
  • Keep the aftercare information
